## The line (186 characters)

> One star. He moved my sunbeam two inches left, sat in my chair, and served dinner in the bowl I already licked clean. I filed a formal complaint by pushing his water glass off the table.

A one-star review written by a house cat about the human it lives with.
Deadpan review shape, complaints only a cat would file — and the formal
complaint is filed the cat way. Three grievances escalate (sunbeam, chair,
bowl), then the punchline: the glass goes off the table.

## Why this line

The brief asks for a review that stays deadpan while the complaint goes
somewhere only a cat would go. Every grievance here is cat-logic: a
sunbeam is property, a chair is a throne, and a licked-clean bowl is
clearly still in use. The "formal complaint" is the joke's second beat —
bureaucratic language for the most cat behavior there is.
